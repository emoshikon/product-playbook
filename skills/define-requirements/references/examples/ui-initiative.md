## 1. Features

### F1: Review suggested matches

**Expected behavior**
- Users can inspect suggested matches and accept or reject a suggestion.
- Users can understand why a match was suggested.

**Requirements and constraints**
- Clearly handle empty, loading, and error states.
- Ensure keyboard access to review actions.

**Acceptance criteria**
- A reviewer can complete a decision and see confirmation.
- Rejected suggestions are not presented as accepted.
