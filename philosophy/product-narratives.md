# Start with a narrative

Before listing requirements, articulate the world after the initiative succeeds. Choose the format that suits the product: a launch announcement, a blog post, a customer story, or a vision memo. There is no mandated template.

Answer: Who benefits? What changes for them? Why does it matter now? What would success look like, including measurable outcomes? What is intentionally outside scope?

Agree on the narrative before debating detailed requirements. Link it from the living initiative document rather than duplicating its content there. Revise it when product direction materially changes.
