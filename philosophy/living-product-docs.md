# Living documents, bounded lifecycles

Traditional PRDs often mix durable product intent with rapidly changing implementation assumptions. Separate them. Each initiative has one living Markdown document with capabilities, a delivery plan, open items, and closure. The document reflects the **latest agreed intent**, not everything ever discussed or necessarily everything implemented.

The PM owns the agreement; engineers own implementation decisions. New information from meetings, PRs, Slack, or Jira can trigger proposed edits. Approval is required for substantive product changes. Git history and linked evidence supply provenance without a compulsory journal.

At GA, reconcile what shipped, document deferrals, and archive. A later iteration starts a new initiative and links back.
